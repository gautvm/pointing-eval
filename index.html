Codex pointing benchmark

Each model gets an image and a phrase and returns one point (POINT: x, y).
Hit = the point lands in the target box.
Every prompt is run 3 times.

63 prompts across 12 LIBERO scenes: bowl-to-plate, pick-into-basket, open-drawer, cleanup.

Ground truth was independently audited by 6 separate reviewers who never saw the model outputs. Misplaced boxes were moved onto the object. Ambiguous prompts were fixed where possible: "the right handle on the open bottom drawer" was reworded to "the handle on the open bottom drawer" (that drawer has one handle) and the models were re-asked; the three "the drawer handle" prompts (3 identical handles) were dropped. Terra's 2 remaining misses are genuine borderline near-misses.
